About me

Hello there! 👋 I'm Oktay, a Full-Stack developer from Berlin. I build web products with React, Next.js, TypeScript and Node.js, and I've taken multiple apps from idea to release on the App Store and Google Play with React Native. My background in graphic design, 3D and photography shows in the details, so my projects work well and look good too.

Many of my projects grow out of things I love doing myself: Drone Pal came from flying drones, and Shape Beats was inspired by years of music production. I use AI tools like Claude Code and n8n every day to move faster, while the architecture and quality decisions stay with me.
