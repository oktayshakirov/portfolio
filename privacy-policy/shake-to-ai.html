Shake to AI Privacy Policy

Shake to AI is an Android voice conversation app developed by Oktay Shakirov. For privacy questions or requests, email shaketoai@oktayshakirov.com.

Voice conversations

When you start a conversation, the app uses your microphone and streams audio to the OpenAI Realtime API over encrypted WebRTC. OpenAI processes the audio and generates the spoken response. If you enter a preferred name or customize the assistant's instructions, that information may be sent during session setup. The app keeps no local recordings, transcripts, or conversation history.

OpenAI describes its data retention, abuse monitoring, and model training practices in its API data controls.

API credentials and optional servers

Shake to AI has no user accounts. If you enter your own OpenAI API key, the app stores it encrypted on your phone and sends it to OpenAI over HTTPS to establish sessions. Your OpenAI account pays for its usage.

If you configure a third-party or self-hosted server, the app sends your device token and session setup data to that server. The server operator controls its data handling. With the project's included token broker, conversation audio goes directly from your phone to OpenAI after the broker issues session credentials.

Phone permissions and storage

The app uses your phone's accelerometer locally to detect shakes. With screen-off shake detection enabled, a foreground service watches for the gesture. The app uses the microphone during conversations and stops capturing audio when they end. Language, voice, and shake settings are saved on your phone.

Reports and support

In versions with AI response reporting, the description you submit goes to Google Apps Script, which emails it to the developer. The app does not attach audio, transcripts, API keys, or device identifiers. The script stores a daily send count to limit abuse. We use and retain these emails as needed to address reports.

If you email us directly, we use and retain your message as needed to respond. If you post a GitHub issue, it may be public and is subject to GitHub's privacy practices. Leave out personal details and API keys when sending feedback.

Privacy requests and deletion

Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove settings and credentials stored on your phone. You can revoke microphone access in Android Settings. Contact the relevant provider for data it holds. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of data we hold about you, email shaketoai@oktayshakirov.com.
